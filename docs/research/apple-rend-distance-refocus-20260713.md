## Confirmed relationships

- `0x012f` remains `1.4`; it is a profile aperture endpoint, not the capture's
  selected simulated aperture. This is no longer inferred only from an
  f/1.4-locked corpus: `IMG_7311...7314` all expose
  `SimulatedAperture=7.1` while their typed `0x012f` records remain `1.4`.
- `0x01c3 / 0x01c2` is `2.5` at 1x and `2.875` at 2x/3x.
- `0x01c4 / 0x01c2` is `0.075` at 1x and `0.0875` at 2x/3x.
- `0x01c5 / 0x01c2` is fixed at `0.5` in this 3x pair, but varies at 1x/2x.
- `0x0192 / 0x0191` is exactly `48` in every capture.
- `0x0193 / 0x0191` is `1.0` at 1x and `0.4` at 2x/3x.
- Both REND families move despite fixed capture aperture. They are focus/scene
  controls, not editor-aperture values.

## Mapping consequence

The Apple renderer cannot be reproduced by selecting a lens profile and one
scene scalar alone. The OPPO converter needs two fitted outputs: a blur-state
family (`0190...0193`) and a focus/scene family (`01c2...01c5`). Raw disparity
at the XMP Focus pixel is not comparable across captures because the relative
disparity gauge changes and the semantic focus component may differ. The input
fit must use OPPO's native-like focus selector, rank quantization endpoints,
focus branch, distance prior and foreground blur scale.

## Matched iPhone/OPPO refocus pairs

Four additional captures hold composition and tap location approximately
constant across devices. The 2x pair taps the mug and the distant headboard;
the 3x pair taps the blue bag and the distant green box.

| 2x far | 7312 | 230059 | 128 | 145 | 1.573242 | 3.009044 | 3.454442 | 4.679308 |
| 3x near | 7313 | 230150 | 146 | 133 | 1.042969 | 9.201309 | 7.334674 | 3.667337 |
| 3x far | 7314 | 230153 | 231 | 183 | 0.287842 | 6.424432 | 5.698077 | 2.849039 |

The named Apple `AFMeasuredDepth` field and OPPO
`rear.depth.config.distance` describe the same capture concept and preserve
near/far ordering in these pairs. XDRemux therefore writes OPPO config offset
296 to MakerApple tag 56 rather than retaining a fixed donor value. The later
134mm 3x-crop pairs show that their numerical scales can differ materially
between devices/scenes (`212/396` versus `123/187`), so this is a semantic
metadata transfer, not proof of an exact cross-device unit calibration and not
an input that can predict REND by itself.

The private REND controls are not described by one global distance multiplier:

- in the 2x pair, moving focus far multiplies OPPO distance by `3.22`, while
  Apple `01c2` increases by `5.22%` and `01c5` decreases by `2.69%`;
- in the 3x pair, moving focus far multiplies OPPO distance by `1.38`, while
  both `01c2` and `01c5` decrease by `22.31%`;
- the 3x `0190...0193` family is unchanged, while the 2x family changes by
  about `5.22%`.

Consequently the product path maps AFMeasuredDepth now, but keeps
profile-representative REND values until each Apple profile has enough
distance/focus samples for a profile-specific fit. Equal-length REND-only A/B
files are generated by `scripts/transplant_portrait_rend.py`; the operation
changes only the 1,804-byte Base64 XMP value and leaves container layout and
all image/auxiliary payloads unchanged.

## 3x physical tele cropped to 134mm

`IMG_7328...7331` are not 5x tetraprism captures. Their EXIF identifies an
iPhone 15 Pro 9mm/77mm-equivalent physical tele camera at 1.734x digital zoom,
producing a 134mm field of view. They therefore extend the measured Apple 3x
profile range from 118mm to 134mm; they do not provide a 5x physical-lens REND
fit.

Across all four captures, every REND record except `0x01c2...0x01c5` is
identical. The varying records obey exactly:

```text
01c3 = 2.875 * 01c2
01c4 = 0.0875 * 01c2
01c5 = 0.5 * 01c2
```

The person scene has nonzero OPPO portrait planes. Its matched near/far pair
moves Apple `01c2` from `7.255665` to `5.478704`, while OPPO internal
focus-disparity/fx moves from `11.740887` to `5.063426`. The generic bed scene
has zero portrait planes; its two matched states have very similar Apple focus
disparity and move `01c2` only from `5.419687` to `5.775743` despite a larger
AF distance change. This identifies semantic focus branch as an input to the
3x scene scalar and rules out fitting `01c2` from equivalent focal length or
config distance alone.
